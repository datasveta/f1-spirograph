We go round and round again...

Each spirograph repeats its circuit's outline once for every Formula One race held there. The more races, the denser the pattern. Tap a circuit to see its winners. Switch to Dynamic and the patterns breathe, folding back into a single track outline and unfolding again.

Race counts and winners: Jolpica F1 API, updated weekly after each race.

Circuit outlines: f1-circuits-svg by Jules Roy (MIT license).
